# Level rules

All levels inherit rules from above them unless contradicted.

## Level 1

These charts will always be Novice difficulty.

## Rhythm

Offbeats disallowed except under obvious circumstances

## Dual actions

Usually only one action at a time across both hands. Dual analog/dual face button is permissible with extreme telegraphing.

Context must remain symmetrical.

## Context changing

At least 1 second between transition from buttons to analog

### Remarks

Stick mechanics are used to differentiate on song segment basis (>~8 bars)

literalyly Fucking NOTHING for the first 4 measures. You can have like, some hold notes that lasts two measures.

increased spacing and duration of individual flavor elements allow physical transition to align with song section change rather
than on a beat-by-beat basis

## Level 2

These charts will always be Novice difficulty.

### Rhythm

Syncopation is allowed: Objects can be placed on non-downbeats (usually eigth notes)

### Dual actions

Dual actions are allowed outside of a syncopated segment.

Dual actions should be used for differentiation on a multi-measure scale.

## Level 3

### Dual actions

Dual actions may be used during syncopated segments.

Dual actions may be on asymmetric contexts if one of the contexts is doing nothing.
